Enemy palette

Every kind is painted from the palette in src/core/palette.js (see docs/enemy-kinds.md). A kind looks the same wherever it spawns. The rosters (DEPTH_SPAWNS in src/core/spawns.js) put it where it fits.

Kinds

Each species' strip runs across depths 1–20. A cell is split between the kinds that depth's roster draws, by weight, and fainter cells are less likely to appear. On each card, the big dot is the body (the anchor of its first role), the small dots are the role's other materials, and the mid-size dots are the other roles. "Turns up in" is the expected number of cubes, summed over depths 1–20, that have at least one of the kind. "Nearest" is the most similar body among the other kinds of the species (OKLab ΔE, red under 0.08). Under it, the ground of every theme the kind spawns in, with the body's ΔE from it: red under 0.04, about the ground's own colour.
