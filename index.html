      Your friends join with this code (up to three):

        Up to four play together, each with their own hero, in the host's world. Friends come and go as they like. Explore together or apart: the coloured dots on the map are your friends, F takes you to them. The monsters go after all of you, every kill gives each of you XP, the loot is shared: whoever gets to it first takes it. Knocked out, you lie there until a friend helps you up. Needs the internet: the browsers find the host through the PeerJS service and then talk to it directly.
