Up to four play together: you play in the host's world and keep your own character. Explore together or apart: the coloured dots on the map are your friends, F takes you to them. Together, the monsters go after all of you and every kill gives each of you XP and loot of your own. Knocked out, you lie there until a friend stands next to you and helps you up. Needs the internet: the browsers find the host through the PeerJS service and then talk to it directly.
